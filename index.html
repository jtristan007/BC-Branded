Who We Are

The mountains
don't care about
your excuses.

BC Branded was born on the trails of the North Shore, in the fog of Squamish, and on the slopes above Whistler. We make clothing for people who push past the treeline — rugged enough for the backcountry, sharp enough for the city. Every piece is designed with the Pacific Northwest in mind, built to handle whatever this coast throws at you.
